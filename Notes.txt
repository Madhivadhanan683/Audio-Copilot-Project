Phase 1: MVP (The "Co-Producer" Essentials)
Chat Interface with Music Context: Speak to the AI about your track naturally.
BPM/Tempo Detection & Sync: AI knows your project's tempo.
Key/Scale Detection & Suggestion: AI recommends keys or detects current key.
MIDI Melody Generation: Text-to-MIDI for melodies (e.g., "Sad violin melody").
MIDI Chord Progression Generation: Generate progressions (e.g., "ii-V-I in C Major").
Drum Pattern Generation: Create beats for specific genres (House, Trap, etc.).
Audio Sample Retrieval: Find samples in your library or online (Mock/API).
Basic Audio Generation: Generate sound FX or short loops (Text-to-Audio).
Project Analysis: Read track count, potential loudness issues.
Settings/Configuration Panel: API Keys, Path management.

Phase 2: Advanced (The "Pro" Features)
Stem Separation: Split mixed audio into individual stems (the feature we were just debugging).
Audio-to-MIDI: Convert hummed melodies or audio clips to MIDI.
Automatic Mixing Suggestions: EQ/Comp curves based on reference tracks.
VST Plugin Recommender: Suggests specific plugins (e.g., "You need a De-Esser here").
Lyrics Generation & Rhyme Assistant: Interactive lyric writing support.
Voice Memo Transcription: Convert recorded ideas/voice memos to notes.
Arrangement Suggestions: AI advice on structure (e.g., "Make the drop longer").
Style Transfer/Humanization: Make MIDI patterns sound like they were played by a human.
Sample Library Auto-Tagging: Automatically organize and tag your local sample folders.
Synth Preset Generation: Create presets for synths like Serum, Vital, or Massive.
Collaborative Session Mode: Allow multiple users or AI agents to work on the same session.
Export/Rendering Assistant: Final checks on loudness/LUFS before exporting.
