MIDI Melody Generation: Text-to-MIDI for melodies (e.g., "Sad violin melody").

Stem Separation: Split mixed audio into individual stems (the feature we were just debugging).
Audio-to-MIDI: Convert hummed melodies or audio clips to MIDI.
Automatic Mixing Suggestions: EQ/Comp curves based on reference tracks.
VST Plugin Recommender: Suggests specific plugins (e.g., "You need a De-Esser here").
